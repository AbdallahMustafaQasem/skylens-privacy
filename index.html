Privacy Policy

App: Sky Lens: Astronomy Map (package aq.store.world)
Last updated: 8 November 2026

Sky Lens: Astronomy Map identifies stars, planets and constellations with your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app works completely offline. It has no internet permission, shows no ads, needs no account and contains no analytics or crash-reporting services, so nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

1. Information used on your device

To work, the app uses the following information on your phone. It is not sent to us or stored on any server:

  • Approximate location (optional) – used to work out which stars and planets are above you. The app asks only for approximate location, never precise location. The last known approximate position is saved on your device so the sky is right the next time you open the app. Location is obtained through the Android location service provided by Google Play services on your phone. Instead of sharing your location you can choose a city or type coordinates, and you can deny or revoke the permission at any time in your phone's Settings.
  • Camera (optional) – used only in camera view, to show the live picture behind the drawn sky. The picture is only shown on screen: the app does not take, save or send photos or video.
  • Motion sensors – the accelerometer, gyroscope and compass are read to know where the phone is pointing. These readings are used immediately and are not stored.
  • Settings – your choices (language, display options, units, location choice and sky alignment) and the last known approximate position are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

The star catalogue and the list of cities are included in the app. Nothing is downloaded while you use it.

2. Data we collect and share

We do not collect any personal data. The app has no permission to access the internet, so it cannot send information off your device. We do not sell or share personal data, and the app contains no third-party advertising or analytics software.

3. Device backup

If you have turned on Android backup, Android may include the app's settings (including the last approximate position) in your device backup stored in your Google account, as it does for other apps. You control this in your phone's Settings, and Google handles these backups under its own privacy policy.

4. Google Play services

Location requests are handled by Google Play services on your phone, which is part of Android on most devices and is covered by Google's privacy policy. The app only receives the position on your device and does not send it anywhere.

5. Data retention and deletion

All data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings → Apps → Sky Lens: Astronomy Map → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.

6. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Security

Because the app has no network access, information stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy: abdallahmqasem@gmail.com
